About Us

SIMPLSYA is a word that represents our vision of simplicity. We aim to offer a straightforward, welcoming space where you can enjoy simple, delicious food and drinks with friends and family. Our rustic grill serves authentic dishes crafted with fresh ingredients and passion. At Zerca's SIMPLSYA Grill & Restaurant, we believe that great food brings people together, creating memorable moments and lasting connections.
